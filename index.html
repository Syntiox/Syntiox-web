SCIENTIFIC MOTIVATION

Bridging the efficiency gap in regional AI.

Standard English-centric tokenizers split South Asian and Brahmic scripts into fragmented byte sequences, inflating token counts and degrading reasoning quality over multi-turn conversations.

Our research explores vocabulary adaptation, localized fine-tuning, and context-aware routing so AI systems understand regional languages with the same speed and precision as global benchmarks.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Research, products & collaboration.

What is Syntiox's primary focus?
Syntiox focuses on AI research, applied intelligence, and scalable systems engineering. We research localized language models (including Sinhala NLP), autonomous agent workflows, and developer APIs, and we deploy those innovations into live public products.
Where can I explore and use Syntiox's live products?
All of our deployed platforms — including Ceylex AI, ATOM MD, API Console, Syntiox Sync, and Game Store — are listed on our dedicated /products page with direct launch links.
How can developers access Syntiox APIs?
Developers can sign in with a Syntiox Account at console.api.syntiox.top or atom.syntiox.top to generate API keys, test endpoints with free trial credits, or upgrade to higher rate limits.
Can I collaborate or request custom AI research & engineering?
Yes. We welcome open-source contributors on our GitHub organization and partner on custom AI solutions, model fine-tuning, and intelligent automation. Reach out via our /support page.
